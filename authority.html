About the authority

Kundan Foundation is a social initiative committed to creating meaningful and sustainable change in society. The Foundation works to support communities through initiatives focused on education, healthcare, food support, social welfare, community development, and other areas of public benefit. Our work is guided by the principles of service, transparency, equality, accountability, and responsible community development. Our Commitment : Kundan Foundation is committed to building trust through responsible service, transparent practices, and meaningful community engagement. We believe that every initiative should create genuine social value and contribute positively to the communities we serve. Service with responsibility. Transparency with accountability. Action with purpose.
